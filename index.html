SignBridge

Talk across signing and speaking

SignBridge reads hand signs from your webcam and turns them into text and speech, and lets you run a few simple tasks by voice. It supports the signs you teach it, not all of sign language.

This browser

    Get started

    1. Open Sign to speech and start the camera.
    2. Under Teach gestures, record at least two signs, twice each.
    3. Start recognition and sign. Letters appear in your text.
    4. Select Speak text to hear it.

    Privacy

    Camera video is processed on your device and is never saved or uploaded. The camera and microphone turn on only when you press Start. Your trained gestures are numbers describing hand shape, kept in memory until you close the page unless you choose to keep them on this device.

    The first camera start downloads the hand-tracking model from the internet. Browser speech recognition may send audio to your browser vendor's service.
